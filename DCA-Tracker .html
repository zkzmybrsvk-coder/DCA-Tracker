▸ How To Use It

Enter each purchase as you make it: the date, the price per BTC you paid, and the amount in sats or BTC. The tool keeps a running average cost — the single price your whole stack breaks even at.

On the chart, each buy is a dot at the price you paid; bigger buys are bigger dots. The orange dashed line is your average cost as it evolved with each purchase. Green dots are in profit at your entered spot; red are underwater. The blue dashed line marks spot.

Saving: when you open this from your Home Screen or a normal Safari tab (the way it's meant to be used once it's on GitHub), every change auto-saves to your device — no action needed, it just persists. The page URL and the Save/Restore code are extra portable backups that survive clearing data or switching phones. Inside Claude's preview device-saving is blocked, so there use Copy Save-Link and reopen that link.
